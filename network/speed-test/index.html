Understanding your results
  • Mbps means megabits per second. Divide by 8 to convert to MB/s. Download and upload tests transfer fixed amounts of data over a single connection and include request round-trip time.
  • HTTP latency is the average round-trip time of 5 requests after a warm-up request. It includes server processing time and is not ICMP ping. Jitter is the average absolute difference between consecutive latency samples.
  • Results reflect the connection from your browser to Cloudflare and depend on Wi-Fi, proxies, background traffic, and server location. Short samples may underestimate fast connections and do not represent your internet provider's maximum bandwidth.
  • If the connection fails, check your network, proxy, or browser blocking settings and try again. Results from completed stages are kept; unfinished measurements show “—”.
